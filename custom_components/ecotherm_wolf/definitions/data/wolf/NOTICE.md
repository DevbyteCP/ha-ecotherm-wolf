# Wolf XML Definitions -- Provenance

The files `device.xml`, `parameter.xml` and `converter.xml` (and the ISM7
client certificate `client.pfx`) are **not** part of the published
integration. Users obtain them from https://github.com/zivillian/ism7mqtt
(see README, `scripts/fetch_wolf_files.py`) and place them in
`<config>/ecotherm_wolf/`.

Copies found here or in `<config>/ecotherm_wolf/` are unmodified upstream
files. Rights to the original content remain with their respective holders
(Wolf GmbH); this project's GPL licence does not cover them.
